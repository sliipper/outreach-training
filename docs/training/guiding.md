# Guiding 
This page contains information on giving guided tours of the LHCb above-ground exhibition and the cavern underground. Linked to this page you can find a draft LHCb 'Guide for Guides' which may be used as a reference and contains quick facts and images of the details you can point out during the visit. Further information on guided tours at point 8 can be found at https://lhcb-outreach.web.cern.ch/visiting-lhcb-at-pit-8/.
